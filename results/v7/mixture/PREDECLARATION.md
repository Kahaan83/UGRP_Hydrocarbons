# Task 11 predeclaration: MolPool mixture heads with the long-cap encoder and nested Optuna (Track C)

Rows: the v6 Track C dev rows (`results/v6/mixture/rows.csv`, role = dev; heads boehm density, boehm ln_visc, kaust mon, kaust ron, leenhouts mon). `guard.assert_dev_only` checks them against `mixture_lock` and `mixture_holdout51`; nothing locked is scored. These rows were already seen in v6 selection, so every gain here is exploratory and cannot reopen the Track C verdict.
Encoder: refit on the Track B pure pool with the v6 config (hidden 300, depth 3, dropout 0.2), epochs 1000, patience 100, 5 models (`pretrain_encoder.py`; the v6 script cannot be imported because it runs on import, so the recipe is reproduced; YSI holdout labels masked), then frozen.
Head: `molpool.fit_predict` on the residual over the same physics baseline as v6, standardised by the training residual SD. Outer folds: GroupKFold(min(5, groups)) on the dev rows, identical to v6. Search: `configs/v7/search_spaces.yaml: molpool` (epochs 2000, patience 150 fixed), nested inner GroupKFold(3), MedianPruner, 60 trials per outer fold, `timeout_s` 1800 per outer fold (budget from `PHASE2_READINGS.md`). Early stopping uses a 15 % group split of the training rows; the final fit per outer fold is a 5-seed ensemble. Unlike v6 there is no "no correction" option; the zero-initialised head returns the baseline at the start and early stopping can stay near it.
Comparators (same rows and folds, read from `results/v6/mixture/dev_oof.csv`): the v6 baselines (best by dev MAE per head) and v6 MolPool (v1).
Metric: OOF MAE per head; paired group bootstrap (4000 draws) 90 % CI of gain = comparator MAE minus v7 MAE; two-sided bootstrap p, Holm over the 5 heads at 0.10, for each of the two comparisons.
Reading rule: "v7 beats the best comparator on a head" only if the Holm p < 0.10 with positive gain; likewise for "beats v1". Otherwise: no evidence of improvement. Heads with few groups (kaust: 5 dev groups, one per outer fold) have very wide intervals; that is reported, not corrected.

# Task 18 predeclaration: equation-parameter heads (Antoine and Andrade) on a D-MPNN

Date 2026-11-22. Data: pure hydrocarbon rows of `measurements_dedup.csv` (vapor_pressure_kPa; viscosity_dyn_mPas), one molecule per system, molecules with 3 or more distinct temperatures per property. Dev only; no lock is read.
Model: `ParamHeadDMPNN(DMPNN)` outputs Antoine (A, B, C) with log10 P/kPa = A - B/(T_K + C) and Andrade (a, b) with ln mu/mPa.s = a + b/T_K. B = 1500 softplus(.), b = 1000 softplus(.) so b > 0 (viscosity falls with T); C = -100 sigmoid(.) (a bounded form of the plan's offset, so T_K + C > 50 K for all data, T_K >= 150). Loss: masked MSE on measured (T, value) points, so one molecule contributes many points.
Evaluation: scaffold GroupKFold (5), val = 15 % of training molecules for early stopping (patience 40, max 300 epochs), 3 seeds averaged. Held-out molecules only.
Comparators: pooled single curve fitted on the training points (Antoine and Andrade), and a ridge on simple descriptors (nC, heavy atoms, rings, aromatic atoms) with 1/T interactions. Reference only, not a comparator: per-molecule refit on the held-out molecule's own points (uses its labels).
Metrics: MAE of log10 P and of ln mu at held-out T, molecule-macro; paired group bootstrap over molecules (4000 draws, 90 % CI), gain = comparator minus model. Reading rule: improves only if the CI excludes 0 with positive gain. Holm over the 2 properties x 2 comparators.
Flash: for held-out hydrocarbons with a pure measured flash point and an LFL in the registry, the v5 route (`predict_flash_le_chatelier`, pure, LFL temp) is evaluated with the head's Antoine constants, the pooled-curve constants, and the registry constants (reference). Trap 3: C10+ paraffins are excluded from the flash evaluation (head and pooled constants are un-refit); the exclusion count is reported. One-sided over-prediction is reported, not only MAE.
Exploratory: dev rows, nothing is adopted into `predict_fuel`.

# Task 10 predeclaration: Track B D-MPNN retrain on the GPU with nested Optuna

Rows and folds: Track B pool, Task 4 folds (full-pool generic-Murcko GroupKFold(5)), ysi_holdout masked. Dev only; v1 stays the declared v6 result and nothing here reopens it.
Model: multi-task D-MPNN (`fuelblend.ml.dmpnn`), search space `configs/v7/search_spaces.yaml: dmpnn` (epochs 1000, patience 100 fixed). Nested CV: outer = the 5 folds, inner = GroupKFold(3) on training groups, objective = mean masked standardised validation MSE, MedianPruner (warmup 20) on inner fold 0. Budget from `PHASE2_READINGS.md`: 60 trials per outer fold, `timeout_s` 5400 per outer fold; trials completed are recorded. Final fit per outer fold: 5-model ensemble with the chosen parameters, early stopping on 15 % of training groups. Debugging reading: the long cap (1000, 100) is required (flash_C gap to Chemprop closed only with it).
Comparators (same rows): v6 v1 OOF (`results/v6/gnn/oof.csv`), the better of Task 6 GBM and RF, and the constant. Metric: OOF MAE per target; paired group bootstrap 90 % CI of gain; Holm over 10 targets at 0.10.
Reading rule: "retrain beats v1 on target t" only if the Holm-adjusted interval of (MAE v1 minus MAE v7) excludes 0 with positive gain. "GNN earns its place vs trees on t" by the Task 6 rule. Otherwise: no evidence of improvement. Because the search was tuned on these dev rows, any gain is exploratory and not a held-out claim.

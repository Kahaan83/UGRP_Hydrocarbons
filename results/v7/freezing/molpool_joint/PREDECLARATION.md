# Task 16 predeclaration: joint pure plus fuel MolPool for freezing (GPU)
Rows: the 52 dev fuels of `results/v6/freezing/features_preds.csv` (holdout never read) plus the pure-compound melting-point rows of the Track B pool (target tm_K, hydrocarbons only, ysi_holdout masked as in Task 10), as single-species "fuels" (x = 1). Fuel set = the 50 mapped species per fuel with mole fractions exactly as baseline b (`s5_inputs`). Embeddings: the Task 11 frozen encoder `results/v7/mixture/encoder.pt` (it was pretrained on the pure Track B pool, including tm_K labels of pure compounds; no fuel label enters it).
Model: `fuelblend.ml.molpool.MolPool` (d 16, rank 8, hidden 32, dropout 0.1, one extra input: task flag 0 = fuel, 1 = pure Tm). Fuel target = freezing_C minus the clipped (-100, -20) v6 `pred_e` (the v6 isomer-tile baseline, as in the v6 step 3 residual), divided by the train residual SD; pure target = Tm in C minus the train mean, divided by the train SD. Loss: weighted MSE with the two tasks given equal total weight. Training: AdamW lr 3e-3, wd 1e-2, batch 64, up to 400 epochs, early stopping (patience 50, best state restored) on a validation split of 15 % of the training fuels (at least 3) and 15 % of the pure compounds; 3 seeds; no hyperparameter search (this is the first joint fit). Uncertainty: MC dropout, 30 stochastic passes per seed, mean and SD over seeds x passes; the prediction is the mean. Curves logged per epoch.
Schemes and comparators: LOGO (52 groups) and leave-one-family-out (4 families, `families.csv`), the same folds and comparators as Task 15 (constant, ridge, GP, UNIFAC-SvL, limiting species, and for LOGO the v6 LightGBM residual), read from `results/v7/freezing/gp/predictions.csv`. Also an ablation without the pure rows (fuel-only MolPool) on the same folds, to isolate the auxiliary pure-Tm loss.
Metrics as in Task 15 (MAE, bias, mean over and under, gain vs constant and vs ridge with 90 % group bootstrap, coverage of the 13.8427 C margin by family). Uncertainty quality: Spearman correlation between the MC SD and the absolute error, and the share of fuels with measured minus predicted <= 13.8427.
Reading rule: the joint MolPool "has skill" only if its gain vs constant and vs ridge have intervals above 0 under both schemes, and "the auxiliary pure loss helps" only if joint beats fuel-only with an interval above 0. Otherwise no skill shown; freezing stays "no skill shown" until a fresh lock (Task 20). Exploratory on seen dev rows.

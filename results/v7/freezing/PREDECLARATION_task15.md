# Task 15 predeclaration: freezing by family, heavy-tail GP and novelty flag
Rows: the 52 dev groups of `results/v6/freezing/features_preds.csv` (the holdout stays unscored; this task never reads `holdout_*`). Families: `results/v7/freezing/families.csv`, committed before any scoring (crc_jeta 23, crc_jeta1 11, ajftd_posf 15, fuellib_reference 3).
Features per fuel (from the mapped GC x GC species and the designated Tm constants used by v6 baseline b): nC14plus_mass, naphthalene_mass, dicyclo_mass, tm_max_present (species with mass fraction > 1e-4), tm_p90_mass (Tm reached when the cumulative mass from the highest-Tm species is 10 %). Model: GP with ARD Matern 2.5 plus white noise on standardised features, normalised target, 5 optimiser restarts.
Schemes: LOGO (52 groups) and leave-one-family-out (4 families, the 3-group family is reported, not interpreted alone). Comparators on the same folds: constant (train mean), ridge on the same features (alpha by inner leave-one-group-out), baseline b (UNIFAC-SvL, no fit) and the limiting-species baseline (Task 14); for LOGO also the v6 LightGBM residual read from `dev_oof_predictions.csv`.
Metrics: MAE, bias, mean over-prediction and mean under-prediction, gain vs constant and vs ridge (per-group MAE difference, 90 % percentile bootstrap, 4000 draws, groups as units). Coverage of the live 13.8427 C margin (share of fuels with measured minus predicted <= 13.8427) by family, with the number of groups behind each figure.
Novelty flag: Mahalanobis distance in standardised feature space against the training rows; threshold = 95th percentile of the leave-one-out scores of the training rows within each fit. Report flagged share, and MAE of flagged versus unflagged fuels under LOFO.
Reading rule: the GP "has skill" only if its gain vs constant AND vs ridge have 90 % intervals above 0 under both LOGO and LOFO; otherwise no skill shown. Whatever the result, freezing stays "status: no skill shown" in the README until a gate is passed on a fresh lock (Task 20). Exploratory on seen dev rows; one primary comparison per scheme; no multiplicity correction.

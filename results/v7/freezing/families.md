# Task 15 family assignment (fixed before any scoring)
Families come from fuel metadata only: CRC AV-33-22 `fuel_type` (Jet A, Jet A-1) in `data/real_fuels/v5/crc_av3322_properties.csv`; AJFTD fuels carry only POSF numbers in their labels, so they form one source family; the three FuelLib fuels (JP-8, JP-5, Jet A reference) form one reference family. No freezing value or model output was used. Counts over the 52 dev groups: crc_jeta 23, ajftd_posf 15, crc_jeta1 11, fuellib_reference 3. A family with 3 groups gives a weak leave-one-family-out fold and is reported but not interpreted alone.

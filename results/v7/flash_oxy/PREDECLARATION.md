# Task 11 predeclaration: flash oxygenate arm with MolPool, long-cap encoder and nested Optuna

Rows: the v6 flash oxygenate dev rows (`results/v6/flash_oxy/rows.csv`, role = dev: 641 oxygenate rows in 57 systems and 95 hydrocarbon rows in 17 systems). `guard.assert_dev_only` checks them against `flash_oxy_lock`; nothing locked is scored. These rows were already seen in v6 selection, so every gain is exploratory and cannot reopen the v6 verdict.
Model: as v6, a MolPool residual over a physics baseline, with the Task 11 long-cap encoder (`results/v7/mixture/encoder.pt`). Arm B trains on hydrocarbon plus oxygenate rows with the baseline chosen in v6 (`chosen_dev.json`, blend law with theta refit on the outer training rows of each fold); arm A trains on hydrocarbon rows only with the linear molar baseline. Folds: GroupKFold(5) on dev system ids, identical to v6. Search: `configs/v7/search_spaces.yaml: molpool`, nested inner GroupKFold(3), 60 trials per outer fold, `timeout_s` 1800, 5-seed final ensembles, 15 % group split for early stopping.
Comparators (same rows and folds, from `results/v6/flash_oxy/dev_oof.csv`): the v6 gate comparator (named in `readings.json`) and v6 MolPool (v1).
Metric on oxygenate rows: MAE; paired system-level bootstrap (4000 draws) 90 % CI of gain = comparator MAE minus v7 MAE; two-sided bootstrap p, Holm over the two comparisons (comparator, v1) at 0.10. Also reported: MAE on systems with at least 3 components, and the one-sided over-prediction (mean over-prediction, 90th percentile of signed error), because over-prediction is the dangerous direction for flash point. Hydrocarbon non-inferiority as in v6: upper end of the 90 % CI of (MAE arm B minus MAE arm A) at most 1.0 degC.
Reading rule: "v7 beats the comparator" only if Holm p < 0.10 with positive gain; otherwise no evidence of improvement. A gain with a worse over-prediction tail than the comparator (90th percentile of signed error more than 1.0 degC above it) is reported as unsafe, as in v6.

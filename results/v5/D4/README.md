## What changed against D3 and why

* D3's v4pool (three n-alkanes, butylcyclohexane, 1,2,4-trimethylbenzene) cannot reach density, freezing and smoke point together;
  the v4pool rows are re-run here and are unchanged.
* trans-decalin was blocked in v4 by two constant statuses, now resolved in `data/processed/v5/pure_props_v5.csv`
  (`results/v5/P1/pure_props_changes_v5.csv`):
  * rho15 `disputed` -> `verified`. v4 compared the listed 869.5 kg/m3 with a thermo-library value of 846.9. Measured ThermoML liquid
    densities (4 temperatures 293-308 K, 4 references) extrapolate to 874.1 kg/m3 at 15 C, 0.52 % from the listed value (threshold 1 %).
    The listed value is kept (R16); it is about 4.6 kg/m3 below the measured one, the conservative side for a minimum-density limit.
  * dHfus `reference_only` -> `verified`: 14.414 kJ/mol (McCullough, Finke et al. 1957, via the NIST WebBook) against the library 14.41.
    The WebBook files this row on the stereo-unspecified decalin page and the cis value on the trans page; assignment to trans is by
    fusion temperature (242.78 K against the trans triple point 242.77 K). The library value probably traces to the same measurement.
* The measured-density reference was applied to all 31 pure compounds with enough data: 27 stay verified, 4 become verified
  (trans-decalin, cis-decalin, heptamethylnonane, bicyclohexyl), none becomes disputed.

## Limits of the result

* Not certified. Freezing has no valid margin (8 independent SLE systems, 9 needed) and the designs sit 0.5-0.7 C inside the limit
  on an ideal Schroeder-van Laar point estimate. Smoke point has no valid margin (6 groups), the model is outside its training
  domain (aromatics 0.3 wt% and 7.1 vol% against 8-23 vol%), and the 25 mm limit is not verified from the standard.
* The v5pool was chosen after seeing the D3 failure (post-hoc pool choice); nothing was measured.
* The Jet A recipe is 0.3 wt% aromatics. D1655 sets only a maximum; synthetic-blend rules (D7566) require at least 8 vol%.
* Independent check: 11 agree, 1 disagrees (Jet A-1 v5pool density, engine 799.5 vs 792.9). The independent value uses thermo's
  trans-decalin density (846.9 kg/m3, the value shown above to be wrong): substituting it into the engine's mixing rule gives 792.6,
  and the measured density gives 800.9. Even the low figure clears the 775 limit after the 4.59 margin.
* The -20 C viscosity margin comes from lab binaries and ternaries; transfer to a 6-component blend is assumed (see D3).

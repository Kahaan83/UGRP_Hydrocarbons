# v5 S1: cold-bin lab viscosity margin (declared BEFORE any score was computed)

## Declared reading (written before scoring)

T_lt_263K and T_263_283K each reach >=9 independent dev systems and get a finite applied_margin in configs/uncertainty_v4.yaml
(generated by the existing script, never hand-edited); inf still means uncertified.

Operationally: `configs/uncertainty_v4.yaml` is NOT edited. The generated counterpart is `results/v5/S1/uncertainty_v5.yaml`,
produced by `scripts/v5/eval_lab_known_composition_v5.py` through the existing `scripts/v4/build_uncertainty_v4.py::add`
(same conformal rule, alpha 0.1, k = ceil((n+1)(1-alpha)), group = system, two-sided |ln(pred/meas)|). A bin gets a finite
margin only if it has >= 9 independent dev systems; otherwise it stays `inf` (uncertified) and nothing is substituted.

## Declared methods (per cold bin; bins T_lt_263K, T_263_283K, T_ge_283K as in v4, edges belong to the warmer bin)

1. **Refutas**: the v4 raw Refutas of `build_viscosity_leaderboard_v4.build_lab` (mass-fraction VBN blending, ideal-volume density).
2. **Arrhenius** (the rule Boehm et al. tested; implemented here as the standard Arrhenius log-mixing rule, which I assume is what is meant):
   ln(eta_mix) = sum_i x_i ln(eta_i), mole fractions, dynamic viscosity, pure eta_i(T) from the same PurePropertyProvider as Refutas.
3. **Walther**: temperature extrapolation with `src/fuelblend/thermo/walther_viscosity.py`. Refutas blend kinematic viscosity is evaluated at
   the warm reference temperatures 288.15, 298.15, 308.15, 318.15 K (the regime where the pure-component fits were measured), Walther
   A and B are fitted to those four points (ASTM D341, log10 log10(nu+0.7) vs log10 T) and extrapolated to the measurement
   temperature; dynamic viscosity = nu * ideal-volume density at that T. No cold-temperature data of the system enter.

Any cold-bias correction (a per-bin, per-method constant offset in ln space) is fit on **dev systems only** (dev residuals use
leave-one-system-out offsets; locked systems use the all-dev offset). The corrected variants are reported separately as `<method>_biascorr`;
uncorrected variants are always reported next to them. **The locked systems are scored once**, in the final run, and are used only for
the counts-only coverage check and the bias/RMSE tables; no choice (method, offset, margin) is made from them.

## Declared data and split protocol

- Added lab table: `data/real_fuels/v5/boehm2022_viscosity.csv` (1470 rows, `measurements_dedup` schema, 23 systems per cold bin per its build log).
  The v4 inputs (`data/unified/measurements_dedup.csv`, `splits/v4/lab_systems_splits.json`, `results/v4/L/*`) are read only; the v5 wrapper
  concatenates the two tables in memory and re-implements the `build_lab` selection/Refutas call (it is regression-checked to reproduce all 15,535 v4 refutas lab residuals to 5e-8).
- Split: the v4 split (`splits/v4/lab_systems_splits.json`, np.random.default_rng(20261111) over the v4 id universe) cannot be recomputed on a larger
  universe without re-drawing existing dev/locked roles, so **every v4 system keeps its v4 role**. A Boehm system that is not in the v4 file is
  assigned by deterministic hash before any residual exists: `int(md5("v5-S1-boehm2022|" + system_id), 16) / 2**128 < 0.20` => locked, else dev.
  The split is written to `results/v5/S1/lab_systems_splits_v5.json`; the script refuses to overwrite a differing file.

## Declared caveats (known before scoring)

- Boehm et al. report **volume** fractions of nested pre-blends; `x_mole` / `w_mass` in the CSV were converted with **15 C pure densities**
  (not the density at the measurement temperature, 233-288 K), and the multi-component systems built from pre-blends assume **ideal additivity**
  of the pre-blend volumes. This adds a composition error of order the cold excess volume/thermal expansion difference (a fraction of a percent
  to ~1-2 % in mass/mole fraction) that is folded into the residuals.
- Pure-component eta_i(T), rho_i(T) at 233-263 K come from the pure-property provider (fits of warm data, extrapolated); the cold residuals
  therefore mix mixing-rule error and pure-component extrapolation error.
- 23 Boehm systems per bin are all binaries / ternaries of a few (~18) hydrocarbons; not 100-component jet fuels.
- Systems are not fully independent in chemistry (shared components across the nested pre-blends), although independent as InChIKey sets.
- The design engine only uses the raw-Refutas margin (`viscosity_kin_cSt.lab_mixtures.raw_refutas.<bin>`); a finite margin for another
  method does not change the D3 design evaluation.

## Outcome (appended AFTER scoring; the declaration above is unchanged apart from the one implementation sentence in the data section)

Full numbers: `RESULTS.md`, `summary_by_bin_method.csv`, `viscosity_lab_residuals_v5.csv`, `uncertainty_v5.yaml`, `lab_systems_splits_v5.json`.
Reproduce: `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/v5/eval_lab_known_composition_v5.py` (split + residuals + yaml), then
`scripts/v4/run_designs_v4.py --out results/v5/D3 --margins results/v5/S1/uncertainty_v5.yaml` and `scripts/v4/verify_designs_v4.py --out results/v5/D3`.

- The declared reading HELD for the raw-Refutas margin: T_lt_263K has 20 dev systems (applied_margin 0.415 ln) and T_263_283K has 16 (0.608 ln); both finite.
- Only 17 of the 23 Boehm systems were scorable: the 6 others contain InChIKey LBNXAWYDQUGHGX, which has no valid pure-property entry in the provider; they are
  dropped by the unchanged v4 validity rule (their roles were still assigned by hash beforehand). 14 Boehm dev + 3 Boehm locked systems enter each cold bin.
- The locked cold slices are tiny (3 and 4 systems): coverage 3/3 and 4/4 for raw Refutas is nearly uninformative.
- Walther extrapolation over-predicts (+0.16 / +0.05 ln dev bias) and Arrhenius under-predicts (-0.14 / -0.10, RMSE up to 0.27-0.37 in the 263-283 K bin);
  raw Refutas is the best of the three in both cold bins on dev RMSE. A dev-only constant offset narrows the T_lt_263K margin (0.415 -> 0.317) but not T_263_283K (0.608 -> 0.617).
- Design re-run (`results/v5/D3`): 0 of 13 certified (same categories as `results/v4/D2`: 7 A / 6 D). The only change is that the four jet kinematic viscosity at -20 C
  constraints change from `uncertified` to `pass`; the other blocking constraints (freezing, smoke point, no model, point-estimate failures) are untouched.
- Scope: in `uncertainty_v5.yaml` only the two cold `raw_refutas` entries are replaced; the warm `T_ge_283K` raw_refutas entry is copied verbatim from v4
  (255 dev systems, 0.1856 ln). (A first assembly had also recomputed it with Boehm, 264 systems / 0.1849; this was reverted as out of scope; scoring and residuals are unchanged by that.)
  The `.arrhenius`, `.walther_extrap` and `*_biascorr` entries (including their T_ge_283K ones, which do include Boehm rows) are comparators only.
- Cold margins are effectively a single-source result: 14 of 20 (T_lt_263K) and 14 of 16 (T_263_283K) dev systems are Boehm systems.
- Walther: for some rows the fitted line hits log10 of a non-positive value (a very low-viscosity component, nu+0.7 < 1) and the prediction is NaN; such rows are dropped, so the
  methods are not scored on identical rows (T_263_283K locked: 46 Walther vs 47 records).
- The Arrhenius definition (x_i-weighted ln eta) is my assumption; neither `data/raw/boehm2022/PROVENANCE.md` nor the workbook text states which Arrhenius rule Boehm et al. tested. UNVERIFIED.
- `scripts/v4/run_designs_v4.py` gained an optional `--margins` flag; with no flag its outputs are byte-identical to `results/v4/D2/*.csv` (checked by re-running into a scratch folder).

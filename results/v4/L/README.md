# v4 review fix 3: known-composition lab residuals (density and raw Refutas viscosity)

Generated by `scripts/v4/eval_lab_known_composition.py`. Residual = predicted - measured. Lab mixtures have measured compositions, so these residuals isolate the physics from the CRC647 reconstruction error.

## Lab split
- 419 structurally-eligible systems (union of density and viscosity lab systems), 84 locked (20 %, `np.random.default_rng(20261111)`), written to `splits/v4/lab_systems_splits.json` BEFORE any residual was computed; the script refuses to overwrite a differing split.
- Margins (`configs/uncertainty_v4.yaml`) use dev systems only; locked systems feed a counts-only coverage check.
- The independent unit for margins is the SYSTEM (distinct InChIKey set), as for the existing lab flash entries.

### Temperature bins
- `t_bin`: `T_lt_263K` (T < 263.15 K), `T_263_283K` (263.15 <= T < 283.15), `T_ge_283K`; the edges belong to the warmer bin.
- The cold bin is thin (see the systems column above): when fewer than 9 independent dev systems exist, the conformal margin is `inf` and no substitute is given.

## Caveats
- Lab data are mostly binaries of n-alkanes/aromatics at 293-323 K; transfer to 100-component jet fuels is not demonstrated.
- Density bias comes from ideal volume mixing (excess volumes neglected); the over/under margins are one-sided on that bias.
- Raw Refutas is validated here on lab binaries, whereas Task 11 found it collapses on CRC647 fuels at -40 C; the lab margin cannot be read as a fuel-level guarantee.

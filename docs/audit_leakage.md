## C3 -- Antoine Baseline Leakage Across Flash LOSO Folds

### C3a -- Shared-Compound Baseline Data-Flow

**Verdict: PASS** (Affected compounds: 13)

13/32 LOSO compounds have pure VP refit data (all 32 have pure Antoine parameters: 13 refit, 19 literature). Severity: LOW / BENIGN. Antoine constants are pure-component thermodynamic properties P_sat_i(T) required for Raoult's law. They contain no blend composition, interaction, or mixture flash data. This does NOT invalidate the LOSO claim.

**Data-flow trace & Severity Assessment:**
- The 31 flash LOSO mixture systems contain 32 unique chemical compounds (InChIKeys).
- 13 compounds had pure-component vapor pressure measurements in ThermoML that were refitted by `refit_antoine.py`.
- All 32 compounds utilize pure-component Antoine constants (13 from `refit_measured`, 19 from `webbook`).
- **Severity: LOW / BENIGN.** The flash physics baseline is Le Chatelier's rule combined with Raoult's law:
  $$\sum_{i} \frac{x_i P_i^{\mathrm{sat}}(T)}{P_{\mathrm{total}} \cdot \mathrm{LFL}_i} = 1$$
- Calculating $P_i^{\mathrm{sat}}(T)$ requires pure-component Antoine parameters $A_i, B_i, C_i$.
- These constants are single-component thermodynamic properties; they contain zero blend composition data, no mixture non-ideality, and no mixture flash measurements.
- The LOSO protocol evaluates model generalization across blend composition space without mixture training data. Holding out pure substance saturation curves would render Raoult's law uncomputable.
- **Conclusion:** Shared pure-component Antoine parameters do NOT invalidate the LOSO claim.

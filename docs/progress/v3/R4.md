## 1. Executive Summary

A foundational insight from Phase E is that **100% of the 11 surveyed published combustion surrogates fail ASTM operational fuel specifications**, whereas all three UGRP Phase E inverse-designed blends pass full-envelope constraints under conformal uncertainty margins.

This divergence is not accidental. Literature surrogates (e.g., Dooley 2nd Gen, Edwards 2-comp, Mueller diesel, TRFs) were engineered exclusively for **gas-phase chemical kinetics**—specifically target H/C ratios, ignition delay times in shock tubes, and laminar flame speeds. Consequently, their formulators sacrificed **liquid-phase physical operability**: cold-flow pumpability (freezing/cloud points), safety margins (flash points), front-end volatility (Reid vapor pressure), and continuous boiling distribution (ASTM D86 distillation).

## 3. Physical Root-Cause Breakdown of Spec Failures

### A. Jet Fuel Surrogates (ASTM D1655)

1. **Edwards 2-Component Surrogate** (n-dodecane / tetralin):
   - *Freezing Point Failure:* Predicted freezing point is **-29.8 °C**, failing the ASTM D1655 limit of **<= -40.0 °C** by over 10 °C. Lacking branched iso-alkanes, binary n-paraffin packing causes rapid solid precipitation.
   - *Viscosity Failure:* -20 °C viscosity reaches **6.89 cSt**, breaching the recalibrated cold-flow envelope.
   - *Aromatic Violation:* Aromatics fraction is **25.5 wt%**, exceeding the 25.0 wt% statutory ceiling.
2. **Bruno-Huber POSF-10325 3-Component Surrogate** (n-dodecane / n-tetradecane / 1,2,4-TMB):
   - *Freezing Point Failure:* Freezing point is **-24.8 °C** (15.2 °C above spec) due to 38 wt% n-tetradecane crystallization.
   - *Excessive Aromatics:* Aromatics reach **31.0 wt%**, creating severe smoke point and seal swell violations.
3. **Dooley 2nd Generation 4-Component Surrogate** (n-dodecane / isooctane / mesitylene / toluene):
   - *Catastrophic Flash Point Hazard:* Predicted flash point is **-1.05 °C**, drastically violating the **>= 38.0 °C** minimum safety standard. Formulators added 22.8 wt% volatile toluene to match flame speed, rendering the mixture an extreme explosion hazard.
   - *Density Deficit:* Liquid density is **765.5 kg/m³**, below the 775.0 kg/m³ minimum requirement.
4. **NIST RP-1 14-Component Surrogate**:
   - *Cryogenic Viscosity:* High decalin and cyclic content push -20 °C viscosity to **7.29 cSt**, failing conservative jet fuel restart guidelines.

### B. Diesel Surrogates (ASTM D975 No. 2-D)

1. **IDEAL & Mueller 4-Component Surrogates** (n-hexadecane / HMN / decalin / 1-MN):
   - *Cloud Point / WAT Failure:* Both surrogates yield cloud points of **+2.9 °C** and **+2.4 °C**, failing the **<= 0.0 °C** winter specification. The 34–35 wt% n-hexadecane fraction triggers early paraffin wax crystallization.
2. **Peugeot 4-Component Surrogate** (n-hexadecane / HMN / decalin / butylcyclohexane):
   - *Density Deficit:* Density is only **804.8 kg/m³** (spec >= 820.0 kg/m³).
   - *Flash Point Deficit:* Flash point is **55.4 °C** (spec >= 52 °C, but fails conformal safety margin).
   - *Zero Aromatics:* Contains 0.0 wt% aromatics, violating elastomer swell and boundary lubrication requirements.

### C. Gasoline Surrogates (ASTM D4814)

1. **Toluene Reference Fuels (TRF-70, TRF-80, TRF-90)**:
   - *Vapor Pressure Collapse:* DVPE is only **2.73 to 3.67 kPa**, far below the **45.0 to 105.0 kPa** required for spark ignition carburetion/cold start.
   - *Boiling Range Distortion:* Only 3 pure compounds generate a stepped, artificial distillation curve.
2. **Co-Optima Core 4-Component Surrogate**:
   - *Insufficient Volatility:* DVPE of **27.83 kPa** remains 17 kPa below the ASTM lower bound.
   - *T50 Distillation:* T50 of **74.03 °C** is below the 77.0 °C minimum, inducing fuel line vapor lock.

## 4. Why Inverse Design Blends Succeed

The three Phase E inverse-designed fuels (**DESIGN_JetA_v2_4comp**, **DESIGN_Diesel_v2_5comp**, **DESIGN_Gasoline_v2_5comp**) solve this multi-objective dilemma by treating thermodynamic, cold-flow, and safety constraints as hard bounds during optimization:

1. **Cold Flow + High Energy Density:** In Jet A, the inclusion of 58% branched isododecane provides liquid disruption that suppresses n-dodecane crystallization, lowering freezing point to **-54.6 °C** while maintaining high heat of combustion (**43.64 MJ/kg**).
2. **Flash Safety + Low Viscosity:** Balancing tetralin (15%) and decalin (15%) achieves a **48.6 °C** flash point (safe above 38 °C) while keeping -20 °C viscosity at **5.99 cSt**.
3. **True Drop-In Viability:** In diesel and gasoline designs, multi-component eutectic freezing point depression, balanced aromaticity (15–36 wt%), and light ends (butane/pentanes) ensure simultaneous compliance with ASTM D975 and ASTM D4814 specifications.

## 5. Conclusion & Recommendations

Published combustion surrogates should **never be used as drop-in fuel surrogates for operability, cold-flow, or logistical safety assessments** without explicit multi-property augmentation. Inverse design with thermodynamic and physical constraints is essential to formulate surrogates that simultaneously satisfy chemical reactivity and ASTM operational certification standards.

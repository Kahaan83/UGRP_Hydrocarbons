## 3. High-Value Datasets for Subsequent Ingestion (Tasks B2 - B4)

1. **CRC FACE Gasolines (FACE A through J)**:
   Published by the Coordinating Research Council (Cannella et al., SAE 2014-01-1964). Contains complete 70-85 peak DHA chromatograms, RON (84 to 102), MON (70 to 93), Octane Sensitivity ($S = 0$ to $12$), DVPE ($45$ to $70$ kPa), density ($700$ to $770$ kg/m³), and ASTM D86 distillation curves. This provides the primary foundation for Gasoline model validation in Phase C/D.
2. **CRC FACE Diesels (FACE 1 through 9)**:
   Published by CRC (Projects AV-04-03 / AV-07-02). Provides GCxGC speciation across 9 fuels designed orthogonally in Cetane Number ($DCN = 30$ to $60$), aromaticity ($15$ to $50$ vol%), and boiling range.
3. **AFRL POSF Reference Jet Fuels**:
   POSF-10325 (A-2 Jet A), POSF-10264 (C-1 Sasol), POSF-10289 (C-2 S-8), POSF-11498 (C-3 Gevo ATJ), POSF-12344 (C-4 HEFA). These fuels span conventional petroleum kerosenes to 100% synthetic renewable paraffins, providing critical blend-of-blends links (Task B4).

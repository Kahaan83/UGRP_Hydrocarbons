> "DeepSets failures on the split test are recorded, not hidden."

$$\alpha_i = \frac{\exp(s_i + \log w_i)}{\sum_j \exp(s_j + \log w_j)}$$

- In linear or bilinear models, the sum $\sum w_i z_i$ is completely invariant.
- In softmax attention, splitting increases the denominator and alters multi-head attention weights unless the network is strictly linear.

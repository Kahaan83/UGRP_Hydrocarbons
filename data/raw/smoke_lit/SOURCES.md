# smoke_lit: literature smoke point / TSI / YSI (v5 ingest, 2026-11-13)

CSVs are generated by `scripts/v5/ingest/build_smoke_lit_raw.py` (numbers transcribed from the PDFs staged in `extras/staging/lit/`, which are
not committed) and read by `src/fuelblend/data/loaders_smoke_lit.py`. Columns: source_id, doi, table_or_page, sample_id, kind, components,
smiles, fractions, fraction_unit (fraction|percent, as printed), fraction_basis (mole|mass|volume|none), property, value, unit, T_C, method,
qualifier, note. Fractions are as printed; the loader divides by the total (handles percent and printed rounding). Page numbers are PDF page
indices unless "printed" is stated.

Check run on the S3 data: every transcribed (MW, SP, TSI) triple satisfies the thesis' own relation TSI = 4.07 MW/SP - 4.8 within rounding
(0 outliers), which cross-checks the typed numbers of Table 4, Appendix A, Appendix B and Tables 7 and 9.

## S3 Mensch 2009 (file S3_mensch2009_smoke_tsi.csv, 228 rows = 114 SP + 114 TSI)
Mensch, A., "A Study on the Sooting Tendency of Jet Fuel Surrogates Using the Threshold Soot Index", MS thesis, Pennsylvania State
University, 2009 (catalog etda.libraries.psu.edu/catalog/9540). No DOI; licence not stated (cited, numbers only).
* Table 4 (PDF p45): 13 pure hydrocarbons, SP and TSI. The thesis text says 16 compounds were measured, but only these 13 are tabulated
  (n-decane and n-dodecane exceeded the 50 mm lamp range; no value given, so not ingested). Iso-octane: the text says it could not be
  measured, yet Table 4 lists 40.0 mm; kept with qualifier `text_conflict`. 1-MN = 100 and MCH = 5 are the scale anchors, so their TSI is
  assigned, not independent.
* Appendix B (PDF p72-73): 6 binary pairs (iso-cetane/1,2,4-TMB, iso-octane/toluene, m-xylene/n-dodecane, 1,2,4-TMB/1-MN, decalin/1-MN,
  MCH/1-MN), 8 to 10 compositions each (45 mixtures), MOLE fractions, SP and measured TSI. Component order = column order of each block,
  verified by recomputing the printed MW. The two pairs on PDF p73 were not in the survey; they are ingested.
* Appendix A (PDF p70-71): repeated measurements of 6 iso-octane/toluene standards (60/40 ... 95/5 by liquid volume), 19+16+1+6+3+4 = 49
  dated replicates, qualifier `replicate` (the thesis gives no mean for these). Ambient temperature and pressure are in the note (missing
  for some dates; where only one of the two columns is present the page order says it is the temperature in C).
* Table 7 (PDF p62): MURI JP-8 surrogates 3-comp A, 3-comp B, 4-comp A with MOLE fractions (Table 3 holds the volume recipes; not used). Table 9
  (PDF p64): JP-8 surrogate (22). These MURI recipes are not in `data/raw/surrogates/fuel_surrogates.csv`.
* Table 8 (PDF p63): JP-8 drum, three occasions (replicate) plus the printed mean (`reported_mean`: SP 23.0, TSI 22); real_fuel, no recipe.
* Not ingested: Table 6 (PDF p53), a rescaled compilation of literature TSI values (secondary data).
* Judgement call: T_C is left blank for all rows (the lamp is run at ambient; ambient values are in notes).

## S4 Rainville/Lemaire 2025 (file S4_rainville2025_smoke.csv, 37 rows)
Rainville, Lemaire et al., Energy & Fuels 2025, 39, 6991, doi 10.1021/acs.energyfuels.5c00385, CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0: numbers only, no text.
* Table 5 (PDF p7): iso-octane/toluene calibration fuels (volume %), SP by manual ASTM D1322, FURTI and VBA (means of 3 tests, +/- in the
  note), plus the ASTM standard value (qualifier `reference`, not a measurement of that paper).
* Table 6 (PDF p9) SP of Jet-A (22.8, no recipe, real_fuel) and surrogates S1-S8, with recipes from Table 1 (PDF p2; checked against the page image).
  Method set to VBA: the paper selects VBA for the later work (section 2.3.3); the table itself does not name the method.
  Table 1 columns: S1, S2 mole (Eddings 2005), S3 and S4 in wt % as headed in the table (S4 is Honnet 2009, mass 80/20 in the repo), S5-S8 mole %.
  sample_id reuses the repo name where the recipe already exists in `fuel_surrogates.csv` (S4 -> Honnet2009_Aachen, S5 -> Dooley2012_2ndGen,
  S6 -> KimVioli2014_UM2; the test checks the system_id matches); others: Eddings2005_S1/S2, AksitMoss_S3, Yu_S7, Rainville2022_S8.
* "xylenes" in S1/S2 is an isomer mix; m-xylene stands in as the structure (flagged in the note).
* Not ingested: calculated Table 2 properties, UI values (computed from SP by a correlation), additive blends (figures only).

## S5 Botero preprint 153 (file S5_botero_smoke_tsi.csv, 13 rows)
Cambridge c4e Preprint 153 (n-heptane/toluene wick-fed flame); no DOI, licence not stated. Table 1 (PDF p8): n-heptane (73.0 mm, a value
the preprint takes from the literature; qualifier `literature_value`, its TSI is derived from it), H90T10, H70T30, H50T50, H30T70 (volume %
toluene per the text on PDF p3 and p5), toluene, gasoline (real_fuel, SP only).

## S6 Boehm 2022 (file S6_boehm2022_smoke_tsi.csv, 16 rows)
Boehm, Yang, Heyne, Energy & Fuels 2022, doi 10.1021/acs.energyfuels.1c03794 (ACS; licence not stated, numbers only).
* Table 5 (PDF p10): n-butylcyclohexane, 1,4-dimethylcyclooctane, 6-component surrogate 1 (mole fractions; printed sum 0.999; cis-decalin
  stored without stereo so the InChIKey set matches decalin elsewhere).
* Table 2 (PDF p5; image-only table, read from a 200 dpi render): column "This work" for 1-MN, MCH, toluene, 1,3,5-TMB and the 40/60 vol %
  toluene/iso-octane fuel. A/B TSI values (100, 5) are assigned anchors, qualifier `anchor_assigned`. The other Table 2 columns
  (Mensch, Olson, Hunt) are compilations of other campaigns and were not ingested (Mensch values are already in S3).
* The Energy & Fuels SI with binary-mixture data is not staged and not ingested.
* Re-check 2026-11-13: a second copy of the article (user download `dot_64807_DS1.pdf`, a US DOT repository copy, staged as
  `extras/staging/lit/DOT64807_DS1.pdf`, sha256 f7cf15709a1af3f0caff2d431ba04b96df379e61b22e8f17c9537ffe66c8fef6, 14 pages) is the same
  article (ASAP version). Its PDF metadata carries the ACS open-access stamp CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so the licence is that, not "not stated".
  Table 5 (text layer) and Table 2 "This work" (rendered page 5) match every row of this file; no new measured data. Tables 3-4 are model
  coefficients; Figures 10-11 (binary mixtures with toluene / iso-octane) are plots only; Table 2 Olson and Hunt columns are secondary.
  Nothing added.

## S7 YSI unified (file S7_ysi_unified_hc.csv, 229 rows of 447)
Das, St. John, McEnally, Kim, Pfefferle, Combustion and Flame 2018; database "Yield Sooting Index Database Volume 2", Harvard Dataverse,
doi 10.7910/DVN/7HGFT8 (v1, 2017-08-24), licence CC0 1.0. Downloaded 2026-11-13 from the Dataverse API (`datafile/<id>?format=original`):
* `ysi_unified/ysi_database_volume2.xlsx` (file id 3048960), sha256 8499be6376633cd94c5be15862893e3e9bbed676c27cad8ddfc4544abf664ccb
* `ysi_unified/detailed_ysi_database_volume2.xlsx` (id 3048959), sha256 5961e0759c396d1adb9727fbc142ead5671e27032624252a9cbc8b102e4bbefb
  (not used by the loader; keeps the original-scale values and the species category)
* Readme.pdf and the primer are local only, not committed.
Hydrocarbon filter: SMILES contains only carbon atoms (rdkit); oxygenates are dropped (218 rows). Property `ysi` = "Unified YSI"; the
unified-scale error is in the note. The database lists some compounds more than once (cyclopentene, cyclohexene, cycloheptene,
1-methylcyclopentene, 1-phenylcyclohexene) from different original campaigns; they are kept as separate rows with the same sample_id (not
aggregated here). Species are pure compounds; no mixture data. T_C blank (flame measurement).

## S8 Yang 2006 (files S8_yang2006_smoke_tsi.csv 46 rows = 23 SP + 23 TSI; S8_yang2006_fuel_class.csv 23 rows)
Yang, Boehman, Santoro, Combustion and Flame 149 (2007) 191-205 (online 2007, file name says 2006), doi 10.1016/j.combustflame.2006.11.007 (Elsevier; licence not stated,
numbers only). Staged as `extras/staging/Jet_fuel_soothing_tendency_Yang_et_al_2006.pdf` (15 pages, sha256 5a280caa0a6f9dec0e61a14a5ba399b78137894010d7180c6e576156376fb4e4). Not previously in the repo (the S6 Boehm 2022 note
mentions only a different paper). Loader `load_smoke_lit` (property rows) and `load_yang_fuel_class` (bulk composition).
* Table 1 (PDF p10): 12 JP-900 candidate fuels (hydrotreated HDT and saturated SAT coal-derived, EI-001..EI-036) plus JP-8 and JP-8+100 (reference): measured smoke point,
  H (wt %), P, N, MA, DA (wt %, MA includes tetralin), MW and TSI. TSI is calculated by the paper from class composition (Eq. 27), qualifier `model_calculated`: it is not an
  independent measurement and must not be treated as one. EI-002 class sums to 99.82 as printed. JP-8 and JP-8+100 have no printed BP or H; JP-8+100 MA printed 12.1.
  Smoke point on an ASTM standard lamp (section 4.2); no recipe or molecule list (kind real_fuel, no InChIKeys).
* Table 3 (PDF p12): 9 Pande and Hardy (Energy Fuels 9, 448, 1995) fuels (Jet A-1 reference, HT3, HC2, AVCAT, HC27, high-naphthene kerosene, North Sea MGO, HC29, ERBS2): SP, H,
  P and N (wt %), MA and DA (vol %), normalized exhaust soot and flame radiation (relative to Jet A-1; HC29 flame radiation 'na'), TSI calculated by Yang. These are secondary
  (quoted from Pande and Hardy), qualifier `secondary_source`.
* Checked twice (rendered page images and the PDF text layer; the text layer of Table 3 drops decimal digits, so Table 3 was read from the images and cross-checked against it).
* Not ingested: Table 2 (TSI of four representative compounds, taken from Olson et al.), Table 4 (R2 values), Figs 3-6 (plots), literature fuel sets behind Fig 4 (Cookson, Ramaswamy).
* Use: bulk smoke-point validation for hydrotreated/saturated coal-derived aromatic-rich fuels (smoke points 5-24 mm, aromatics up to 98 %), a range not covered by AJFTD jet fuels.
  The paper's TSI-from-composition rule (TSI = 3.18 MW/SP, a = 3.52, b = -7.42 for the 14 JP-900 fuels in Fig 3) is a fit, not data.
